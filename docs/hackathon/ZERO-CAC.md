# Zero-CAC growth strategy

## Organic sharing loop

1. A developer builds and publishes a portfolio from approved public GitHub projects.
2. They share the public link with recruiters, clients, peers, or communities.
3. The portfolio's visible “Create your own portfolio” invitation offers those visitors the same GitHub-to-portfolio workflow.
4. A referral link records the originating published portfolio across a later GitHub sign-up, when the application can identify a genuinely new account.
5. Owners can review their own deduplicated funnel in Analytics.

## Attribution and event definitions

- **Portfolio view browser-day:** one `view` event per published portfolio, pseudonymous browser token, and UTC day. Private previews are not instrumented.
- **CTA click browser-day:** one `cta_click` event per published portfolio, pseudonymous browser token, and UTC day, generated by the existing referral route.
- **Unique referred registration:** a newly created account attributed to a valid signed referral cookie, if its 30-day expiry has not passed, the source portfolio remains published, and the account is not the portfolio owner. A database uniqueness constraint makes the account conversion idempotent.
- **Registration-to-click rate:** unique referred registrations divided by CTA click browser-days, displayed as a descriptive ratio. This is not a same-person cohort conversion and can exceed 100% because registrations are account-based while clicks are daily browser counts.

The attribution cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production, signed with the server-side NextAuth secret, and cleared after a successful sign-in. A newer valid CTA overwrites an older attribution (last valid click wins). Analytics writes are best effort and do not block rendering or authentication.

## Paid spend and CAC

The dashboard's “$0 reported” is an assumption that no paid acquisition spend has been entered. It describes the intended organic strategy; it is not evidence that acquisition, infrastructure, or operating costs are zero. Customer acquisition cost should ultimately include measured sales and marketing costs divided by acquired customers, with a defined time window and attribution policy. SkillLoop currently does not collect a complete cost ledger or claim measured CAC.

## Limitations

- Browser-days are pseudonymous estimates, not verified unique people; cookie deletion, shared devices, multiple browsers, bots, and concurrent first visits can affect counts.
- Visitor and request throttling is process-local and therefore not a global quota across server instances.
- Clicks and registrations are not a cohort denominator/numerator pair.
- Referred-user portfolio publication outcomes are not tracked.
- Analytics storage failures are intentionally swallowed; events can be lost.
- A real new-user OAuth conversion requires an actual successful OAuth flow and a database write; automated fixtures do not prove external GitHub acceptance.

## Future growth opportunities

Test clearer share prompts, referral landing copy, public examples, developer community partnerships, and owner-requested reminders. Add cohort measurement and trustworthy cost reporting only after privacy, retention, and denominator definitions are designed. Avoid incentivized referrals until abuse controls and economics are validated.
